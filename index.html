How to use Lucky Pick

Type or paste a list of names into the box, one name per line. Choose how many winners you want, then press Pick a Random Name. The randomizer shuffles through names and locks on the first winner — then press Pick Next for each additional winner.

Pick multiple winners without repeats

Set “Winners” to 2 or more. Each name can win only once. After every pick, the winner is removed from the remaining list so the next pick is always fair.

Realistic prize-wheel sounds

Enjoy continuous spinning whoosh with rhythmic peg ticks that slow down naturally, a satisfying winner chime, and a short applause celebration — just like a real prize wheel. Adjust volume or mute anytime.

Great for giveaways, classrooms & teams

Use it for social media giveaways, raffles, choosing who goes first, picking a student, or deciding who buys lunch. It’s free, needs no signup, and works on phones, tablets and computers.
